# Type Guide — Landing Page / Website

## Craft rules

- One job, one action. The page exists to do one thing. Every
  section must earn its place by serving that job or answering the
  named objection. A section that serves neither is removed.
- Hero first: the most characteristic thing opens the page — the
  headline, the product, the proof, the demo. Headline at most 2
  lines on desktop, subtext at most 20 words, CTA visible without
  scrolling.
- Proof before pitch, where trust is the objection: real numbers,
  real testimonials with names, real client logos with permission,
  placed where the doubt appears — not buried at the bottom.
- Sections vary in composition. The same card row repeated down the
  page is a template, not a design. Vary the grid, the scale, and
  the rhythm — deliberately, and stated in `DESIGN.md`.
- Real content in every section. No lorem, no empty frames, no
  "coming soon" sections in a finished page.
- Forms are real: a destination, a success state, an error state.
  Never a fake "thanks" form.
- Technical craft: renders at 320px, 768px, and 1280px; no overflow;
  contrast AA; visible focus; `prefers-reduced-motion` honoured;
  meta, Open Graph, and SEO fields filled from the intake.
- Performance is design: a page that loads slowly is a bad design,
  however it looks in a screenshot.

## Anti-slop for web

- No centred hero plus two buttons plus three identical cards. No
  gradient washes, no glowing blobs, no glassy cards by default.
- No ALL-CAPS eyebrow above every heading, no single accented word
  in the headline, no icon above every heading.
- No invented stats, testimonials, or logos. No "Trusted by" row
  without permission and a source.

## Research notes for web

Research Awwwards Sites of the Day and the free galleries in the
research playbook for this niche. Extract: the hero idea, the proof
placement, the section rhythm, the type scale. Record what was not
copied — especially distinctive compositions and copy.

## Deliverable

Working page or pages (code or a built file, as agreed), screenshots
at 3 widths, the DESIGN.md, and the QC result.
