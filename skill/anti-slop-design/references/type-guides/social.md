# Type Guide — Social Media Post

## Craft rules

- One message in 10 words or fewer on the image. The caption carries
  the rest. If the image needs a paragraph, it is two posts.
- Design for the feed first: the post must read at thumbnail size,
  in a grid next to the brand's other posts, and on a phone held
  one-handed.
- Text legibility: real contrast on real photos, safe areas
  respected, no small text that vanishes at feed size. On-image text
  sized up, never shrunk to fit.
- Format is part of the design: 1:1, 4:5, 9:16, and 16:9 are
  different compositions, not the same file cropped. Recompose per
  ratio.
- Carousels: one idea per slide, a reason to swipe, a payoff on the
  last slide. A carousel that repeats the same layout four times is
  one slide repeated.
- The CTA is one action, stated once, in the caption and (where it
  fits) on the image. Never two competing CTAs.
- Captions and hashtags: use the approved text exactly. Never invent
  a claim, a price, or a date in a caption.

## Anti-slop for social

- No generic gradient background with centred white text and a stock
  photo. No "AI-looking" set: swirly gradients, pastel everything,
  the same avatar or prop in every slide.
- No tiny text, no text crammed to the edges, no low-contrast
  pastel-on-white.
- No invented engagement bait ("Comment YES") unless the intake
  explicitly asks for it.

## Research notes for social

Research the platform's top accounts in this niche and the brand's
own grid. Extract: hook in the first line, type size at feed scale,
how the grid holds together, what makes a post saveable or shareable.
Record what was not copied — especially other accounts' distinctive
layouts and copy.

## Deliverable

Image files at the exact ratios requested, ready-to-paste caption
and hashtags, and a note of the one message and one CTA.
