# Type Guide — Ad Creative

## Craft rules

- The objective decides the design. A click ad, an install ad, and
  an awareness ad are different pieces. Name the objective and the
  KPI in `DESIGN.md` before designing.
- Hierarchy in an ad is brutal and short: hook, proof or offer, one
  CTA. The viewer gives it about a second. The hook must be the
  biggest thing on the piece.
- Approved copy is law. Headline, body, CTA, offer, price, deadline,
  and disclaimer are used exactly as approved. Never rewrite legal
  copy, never invent a discount, never invent urgency.
- One concept, many sizes — and separately, many hooks. Do not
  confuse resizing with testing. Each placement gets its exact pixel
  size, recomposed, not stretched.
- Proof beats adjectives. A real rating, a real number, a real review
  with a name — with permission — outperforms "best" and "amazing"
  every time.
- Brand and compliance constraints are checked before delivery:
  claims allowed, claims banned, disclaimers present, no
  before-and-after where banned, no medical or financial promises
  without approval.

## Anti-slop for ads

- No generic gradient background, stock handshake photo, and "Unlock
  your potential" headline. No invented "Trusted by 10,000+ teams".
- No fake urgency ("ends tonight") unless the intake gives a real
  end date. No fake countdown, no fake notification, no fake chat
  bubble.
- No tiny legal text that contradicts the big claim.

## Research notes for ads

Research the competitors' ads named in the intake and the top ads
in the niche. Extract: the hook, the proof, the offer structure,
the CTA. Record what was not copied — especially competitors'
headlines, imagery, and distinctive layouts.

## Deliverable

Creatives at every requested size, the approved copy used verbatim,
the offer and deadline as given, and a list of the variants (sizes
and hooks) delivered.
