# DESIGN.md — Linear (observed analysis)

> Hand-written analysis for this skill, from Linear's public site and
> product. Tokens are observed / inferred hypotheses, not an official
> brand document. Do not copy Linear's UI, copy, logo, or layouts.
> Source: https://linear.app

## 1. Overview
Linear's system is built for people who live in the product: fast,
dark, precise, keyboard-first. The brand is the feeling of speed.

## 2. Brand personality
Focused, technical, calm under pressure. No noise, no marketing
shout.

## 3. Colour (observed)
- Background: near-black `#08090A` / `#0F1011`, surfaces slightly
  lighter.
- Text: off-white `#F7F8F8`, muted grey `#8A8F98`.
- Accent: a restrained indigo-violet used for the active state only,
  never as a wash. Borders are hairline greys.
- Rule: dark is a working environment, not a fashion choice.

## 4. Typography
- Display and body: Inter-like grotesque, small sizes, tight
  leading, clear hierarchy by weight.
- Monospace for issue IDs and code-like data.

## 5. Spacing and layout
- Dense but ordered: 4px grid, consistent 8px rhythm, panels and
  lists rather than cards everywhere.

## 6. Components
- Command palette, keyboard shortcuts, status pills that mean a
  real state. Every component has hover, focus, and active states.

## 7. Imagery and iconography
- Product UI itself is the imagery. Icons are thin, consistent,
  one weight.

## 8. Motion
- Fast, short, purposeful: 100–200ms transitions that communicate
  state change, never decoration.

## 9. Voice and copy
- Short, technical, respectful of the user's time. Feature names
  describe the job.

## Do / Don't
- Do: design states first, keep motion fast and meaningful.
- Don't: copy the dark theme, the issue UI, or the copy. Dark plus
  violet is a banned reflex unless the brief's subject earns it.
- Take for your work: density as a decision, motion as state.
