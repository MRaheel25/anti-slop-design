# DESIGN.md — Vercel (observed analysis)

> Hand-written analysis for this skill, from Vercel's public site.
> Tokens are observed / inferred hypotheses, not an official brand
> document. Do not copy Vercel's logo, copy, or layouts.
> Source: https://vercel.com

## 1. Overview
Vercel's system is black and white with one geometric idea: the
triangle. Speed is the brand, and the design is the proof — fast,
sharp, minimal.

## 2. Brand personality
Sharp, fast, developer-first. Confident without decoration.

## 3. Colour (observed)
- Background: black `#000000` or white `#FFFFFF` as fields.
- Text: white on black, black on white; muted grey `#888888`.
- Accent: almost none. Colour appears in product screenshots, not
  in the brand chrome.
- Rule: monochrome is the system; colour must earn its place.

## 4. Typography
- Display: Geist, large, tight, confident.
- Body: Geist, regular, short lines. Code in Geist Mono.

## 5. Spacing and layout
- Strong grid, generous whitespace, triangular motifs used sparingly
  as a brand mark, not as a pattern fill.

## 6. Components
- Minimal buttons: white on black or black on white, small radius.
- Cards are plain, bordered, no shadow.

## 7. Imagery and iconography
- Product screenshots and code, presented plainly. The triangle
  mark appears once, large, or not at all.

## 8. Motion
- Fast and subtle. Nothing animates without a reason.

## 9. Voice and copy
- Short, technical, direct. "Develop. Preview. Ship." — three verbs,
  each a real job.

## Do / Don't
- Do: commit to monochrome, let one geometric idea carry the brand.
- Don't: copy the triangle mark, the black hero, or the copy.
- Take for your work: one idea, held everywhere, colour rationed
  to zero unless it means something.
