# DESIGN.md — Duolingo (observed analysis)

> Hand-written analysis for this skill, from Duolingo's public site
> and app. Tokens are observed / inferred hypotheses, not an official
> brand document. Do not copy Duolingo's characters, copy, logo, or
> layouts. Source: https://www.duolingo.com

## 1. Overview
Duolingo's system is a game you can learn in: chunky, friendly,
character-led. Green is a promise — a streak, a lesson done — not
a decoration.

## 2. Brand personality
Friendly, encouraging, a little silly. It cheers you on and never
punishes.

## 3. Colour (observed)
- Background: white `#FFFFFF`, soft green tints for success.
- Text: dark slate `#3C3C3C`, muted grey.
- Accent: green `#58CC02` for the primary action and progress;
  a small set of flat, bright colours (blue, red, yellow) each
  mean one game state.
- Rule: colour means a state or a reward, never a wash.

## 4. Typography
- Display: a rounded, chunky grotesque, bold, large.
- Body: the same family, regular, short sentences.

## 5. Spacing and layout
- Big touch targets, generous spacing, one task per screen, a clear
  progress bar at the top. The lesson is the layout.

## 6. Components
- Chunky buttons with a hard shadow (a deliberate, flat, game-like
  depth), progress bars, streaks, and character reactions.

## 7. Imagery and iconography
- Characters (the owl and friends) carry the personality. Icons are
  chunky, flat, consistent.

## 8. Motion
- Bouncy, short, rewarding: a correct answer celebrates; a wrong
  answer shakes gently and explains.

## 9. Voice and copy
- Short, encouraging, second-person. "You did it!" — a real event,
  not a slogan.

## Do / Don't
- Do: make progress visible, make the character earn its place.
- Don't: copy the owl, the green, or the game UI. Chunky plus green
  is not a Duolingo costume.
- Take for your work: state as colour, progress as the layout.
