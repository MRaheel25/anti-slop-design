# DESIGN.md — Notion (observed analysis)

> Hand-written analysis for this skill, from Notion's public site and
> product. Tokens are observed / inferred hypotheses, not an official
> brand document. Do not copy Notion's UI, copy, logo, or layouts.
> Source: https://www.notion.com

## 1. Overview
Notion's system is paper-like and quiet: monochrome, text-first, one
accent. The product is writing, so the brand is writing.

## 2. Brand personality
Calm, organised, a little playful underneath. It respects the user's
words.

## 3. Colour (observed)
- Background: white `#FFFFFF`, warm off-white `#F7F6F3`.
- Text: near-black `#37352F`, muted `#787774`.
- Accent: red `#E03131` used very sparingly, plus a small set of
  muted highlight colours that mean a label, not a decoration.
- Rule: monochrome first; colour is a label.

## 4. Typography
- Display and body: a grotesque with generous line height; serif
  available as a user choice, not imposed.
- Hierarchy by size and weight; blocks and toggles structure text.

## 5. Spacing and layout
- Narrow text column, generous margins, blocks with clear rhythm.
  The page is the product.

## 6. Components
- Plain buttons, toggles, checkboxes, and blocks. No shadows, no
  gradients, hairline borders.

## 7. Imagery and iconography
- Simple black-and-white line icons, one weight. Illustrations are
  minimal and human, used rarely.

## 8. Motion
- Almost none. The product moves when the user moves something.

## 9. Voice and copy
- Plain, helpful, second-person. "Write, plan, share." Verbs, not
  adjectives.

## Do / Don't
- Do: let text and structure carry the design, ration colour to labels.
- Don't: copy the block UI, the icons, or the copy.
- Take for your work: quiet as a decision, text as the product.
