# DESIGN.md — Aesop (observed analysis)

> Hand-written analysis for this skill, from Aesop's public site and
> stores. Tokens are observed / inferred hypotheses, not an official
> brand document. Do not copy Aesop's photos, copy, logo, or layouts.
> Source: https://www.aesop.com

## 1. Overview
Aesop's system is restraint as luxury: amber glass, quiet type,
generous space, and no shouting. The product's material is the
brand.

## 2. Brand personality
Quiet, considered, literary. It trusts the reader to pay attention.

## 3. Colour (observed)
- Background: warm off-white `#F6F1E7`, stone `#E8E0D0`.
- Text: near-black `#333333`, muted brown-grey.
- Accent: amber brown `#8A6D3B` and the amber of the glass itself.
  No bright accent, no gradient.
- Rule: colour comes from the material (amber glass, stone, wood),
  not from a palette chosen for impact.

## 4. Typography
- Display: a refined serif for headlines, used sparingly.
- Body: a clear grotesque, generous line height, long-form copy
  allowed to breathe.
- Hierarchy by size and by the serif's presence, not by decoration.

## 5. Spacing and layout
- Very generous whitespace, narrow text columns, product bottles
  photographed large and plainly. One product per view.

## 6. Components
- Minimal buttons: text links and quiet bordered buttons. No pills
  everywhere, no icons above headings.

## 7. Imagery and iconography
- Product photography on plain, warm backgrounds. No lifestyle
  stock, no illustration as decoration.

## 8. Motion
- Slow and minimal. The site moves like a person turning a page.

## 9. Voice and copy
- Long, literary, specific: ingredients, places, rituals. It
  explains what is in the bottle and why, in full sentences.

## Do / Don't
- Do: let material and space carry luxury, write in full sentences.
- Don't: copy the amber, the serif, or the copy. Quiet is a decision,
  not a template — warm cream plus serif is a banned reflex unless
  the subject earns it.
- Take for your work: restraint as luxury, material as colour.
