# DESIGN.md — Airbnb (observed analysis)

> Hand-written analysis for this skill, from Airbnb's public site.
> Tokens are observed / inferred hypotheses, not an official brand
> document. Do not copy Airbnb's photos, copy, logo, or layouts.
> Source: https://www.airbnb.com

## 1. Overview
Airbnb's system is photography-led and human: real homes, real hosts,
one coral accent. Warmth comes from the photos, not from decoration.

## 2. Brand personality
Warm, welcoming, human. It feels like a host, not a hotel chain.

## 3. Colour (observed)
- Background: white `#FFFFFF`, light warm grey `#F7F7F7`.
- Text: near-black `#222222`, secondary `#717171`.
- Accent: coral red `#FF385C` for the primary action and the logo
  only — used sparingly, never as a wash.
- Rule: one accent, one job (the action). Everything else is neutral.

## 4. Typography
- Display: a rounded grotesque, friendly but not childish.
- Body: the same family, regular, generous line height.
- Hierarchy by size; prices and ratings are set in a clear, scannable
  way.

## 5. Spacing and layout
- Photo-led cards in a grid, generous gutters, search as the hero.
  The photo is the biggest element on every card.

## 6. Components
- Pill search bar, rounded buttons, cards with a photo, a title,
  and a price. No icon above every heading.

## 7. Imagery and iconography
- Real photography of real homes, shot bright and honest. Icons are
  simple and consistent.

## 8. Motion
- Subtle: photos scale slightly on hover, the search bar expands.
  Motion never distracts from booking.

## 9. Voice and copy
- Warm, plain, specific. Place names, real details, host names.
  "Stay somewhere unique" is a promise the photos keep.

## Do / Don't
- Do: let real photography carry warmth, use one accent for one job.
- Don't: copy the coral, the card layout, or the copy. Coral plus
  photography is not a template to reuse.
- Take for your work: warmth from real images, accent rationed to
  the action.
