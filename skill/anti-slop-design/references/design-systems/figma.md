# DESIGN.md — Figma (observed analysis)

> Hand-written analysis for this skill, from Figma's public site.
> Tokens are observed / inferred hypotheses, not an official brand
> document. Do not copy Figma's illustrations, copy, logo, or layouts.
> Source: https://www.figma.com

## 1. Overview
Figma's system is playful precision: colourful, but disciplined.
Colour is used in blocks and shapes, never in gradients, and the
community's work is the proof.

## 2. Brand personality
Creative, collaborative, a little mischievous. It is a tool for
makers, and it looks like one.

## 3. Colour (observed)
- Background: white `#FFFFFF`, dark sections in near-black.
- Text: black `#000000` on white (used as a deliberate field), white
  on dark.
- Accents: a small set of flat, saturated colours — green, orange,
  purple, blue — each used in solid blocks, never blended.
- Rule: flat colour in solid shapes; no gradients, no glows.

## 4. Typography
- Display: a bold grotesque, large, tight.
- Body: the same family, regular. Code and UI labels in monospace
  where they are data.

## 5. Spacing and layout
- Bold blocks of colour, generous whitespace, playful shapes that
  frame real product UI. The product screenshot is always present.

## 6. Components
- Solid buttons, clear cards, community files presented as real
  work, not as decoration.

## 7. Imagery and iconography
- Flat, colourful illustrations and real community designs. Icons
  are simple, geometric, consistent.

## 8. Motion
- Playful but short: shapes move to show collaboration (cursors,
  multiplayer), not to decorate.

## 9. Voice and copy
- Direct, maker-to-maker. "Design, prototype, and collaborate" —
  verbs for real jobs. Community names and work are credited.

## Do / Don't
- Do: use flat colour in solid shapes, show real work as proof.
- Don't: copy the shapes, the illustrations, or the copy. Flat
  colour blocks are a technique, not a Figma costume.
- Take for your work: colour disciplined into blocks, community
  proof over adjectives.
