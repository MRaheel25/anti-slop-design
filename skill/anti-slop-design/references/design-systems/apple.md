# DESIGN.md — Apple (observed analysis)

> Hand-written analysis for this skill, from Apple's public site and
> product pages. Tokens are observed / inferred hypotheses, not an
> official brand document. Do not copy Apple's photos, copy, logo,
> or layouts. Source: https://www.apple.com

## 1. Overview
Apple's system sells the product by removing everything else. One
product, one idea, enormous space, enormous type.

## 2. Brand personality
Quiet, confident, premium. It never shouts, never explains at length,
never uses more than one idea per screen.

## 3. Colour (observed)
- Background: near-white `#F5F5F7`, pure black sections `#000000` used
  deliberately as a field, not as a default.
- Text: near-black `#1D1D1F`, secondary grey `#86868B`.
- Accent: blue `#0071E3` for links and CTAs only.
- Rule: colour is a field or a single action colour, never a wash.

## 4. Typography
- Display: San Francisco, very large, tight tracking, light weights
  for headlines.
- Body: San Francisco, regular, generous line height.
- Hierarchy by size jumps, not by decoration. No eyebrow on every
  section.

## 5. Spacing and layout
- Huge vertical rhythm, centred compositions, one column for hero.
- Product photography is the layout: the image carries the section.

## 6. Components
- Minimal buttons: filled blue pill for the primary action, plain
  text link for the secondary. No icon above every heading.

## 7. Imagery and iconography
- Real product photography on plain backgrounds, shot large.
- No stock photography, no illustration as decoration.

## 8. Motion
- Slow, smooth scroll-driven motion. Motion shows the product, never
  decorates the page.

## 9. Voice and copy
- Short, plain, confident. "Shot on iPhone." One claim per line.
- No superlatives without a product fact behind them.

## Do / Don't
- Do: spend space, use one idea per screen, let the product be the image.
- Don't: copy the black hero, the product shot composition, or the copy.
- Take for your work: restraint as a decision, size as hierarchy.
