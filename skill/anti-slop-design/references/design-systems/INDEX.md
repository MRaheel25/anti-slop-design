# Design Systems Index — read this first, then 1–2 files only

These are hand-written analyses of public design languages, written
for this skill from public sites, product screenshots, and brand
guidelines that are openly published. Tokens are **observed and
inferred hypotheses**, not official brand documents — check the
brand's own guidelines before shipping anything that claims to be
that brand. Never copy a logo, photo, piece of copy, or distinctive
layout from these brands. Use a system for a *named dimension only*
(for example: "Stripe — proof and pricing clarity"), and record what
was intentionally not copied.

How to use this index: pick the feel your brief needs, read the one
or two closest files, take one principle and one mechanism from each,
then close the files and design from your own brief.

## Calm and editorial

- **Apple** — `apple.md` — restraint, product photography, huge type, empty space as luxury.
- **Airbnb** — `airbnb.md` — warm, human, photography-led, one coral accent used sparingly.
- **Notion** — `notion.md` — quiet, paper-like, monochrome with one accent, text as the product.

## Precise and technical

- **Stripe** — `stripe.md` — proof before pitch, crisp diagrams, restrained colour, developer trust.
- **Linear** — `linear.md` — dark, precise, keyboard-first, motion that communicates state.
- **Vercel** — `vercel.md` — black and white, triangular geometry, speed as the brand idea.
- **Figma** — `figma.md` — playful precision, colourful but disciplined, community and craft.

## Warm and playful

- **Spotify** — `spotify.md` — black plus one vivid green, bold display type, music as colour.
- **Duolingo** — `duolingo.md` — chunky, friendly, game-like, character-led, green as a promise.
- **Monzo** — `monzo.md` — hot coral as identity, plain-English copy, friendly fintech.

## Functional and tactile

- **IKEA** — `ikea.md` — blue and yellow as a system, flat illustration, instructions as design.
- **Aesop** — `aesop.md` — amber glass, quiet typography, restraint as luxury, no shouting.

## Choosing

- Need trust and clarity? Start with Stripe, then Notion.
- Need warmth without noise? Start with Airbnb, then Aesop.
- Need energy without slop? Start with Spotify or Duolingo, then
  check the banned list — energy is the easiest place to slip into
  neon and gradients.
- Need a product UI? Start with Linear or Vercel, then Figma.

Do not read all 12 files. Read the index, pick 1–2, take the named
dimension, and move on to Gate 3.
