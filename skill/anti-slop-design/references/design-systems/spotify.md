# DESIGN.md — Spotify (observed analysis)

> Hand-written analysis for this skill, from Spotify's public site and
> app. Tokens are observed / inferred hypotheses, not an official
> brand document. Do not copy Spotify's artwork, copy, logo, or layouts.
> Source: https://www.spotify.com

## 1. Overview
Spotify's system is black plus one vivid green, with bold display
type. Music is the colour: album art supplies the variety, the chrome
stays black.

## 2. Brand personality
Bold, musical, direct. It is confident enough to let the art do the
talking.

## 3. Colour (observed)
- Background: black `#121212`, darker black `#000000`.
- Text: white `#FFFFFF`, muted grey `#B3B3B3`.
- Accent: green `#1ED760` for the primary action only.
- Rule: the chrome is black; colour comes from the content (album
  art), not from the brand.

## 4. Typography
- Display: a heavy grotesque, very large, tight — the headline is
  the poster.
- Body: the same family, regular, short lines.

## 5. Spacing and layout
- Bold posters, large type, generous space, grids of album cards.
  One idea per poster.

## 6. Components
- Green pill button for the primary action, plain text links for
  everything else. Cards are album art plus a title.

## 7. Imagery and iconography
- Album art and artist photography, real and varied. Icons are
  minimal and consistent.

## 8. Motion
- Short and rhythmic: hover states, play transitions. Motion follows
  the music's beat, never decorates.

## 9. Voice and copy
- Short, bold, direct. "Music for everyone." One claim, kept by
  the catalogue behind it.

## Do / Don't
- Do: let content supply colour, use type as the poster.
- Don't: copy the green, the black, or the poster layouts. Black
  plus green is a banned reflex unless the brief earns it.
- Take for your work: chrome stays quiet, content supplies colour.
