# Intake — Logo

Ask ALL questions in ONE message. Do not design with gaps. Record
unanswered items as explicit assumptions to confirm, never as silent
inventions. Required questions are marked [R].

## Universal (every type)

- [R] What is it for — what is the one action the viewer should take
  after seeing this?
- [R] Who is it for (audience, age, context, where they will meet it)?
- [R] Where will it be seen (sizes, surfaces, light and dark contexts)?
- [R] What exists already (brand, logo, colours, fonts, photos, copy)?
  Attach files or links.
- [R] What must it NOT look like, and who are the competitors to
  differ from?
- [R] What is the deadline and the deliverable format (file types,
  sizes, variants)?
- What is unknown — and who can answer it?

## Logo-specific

- [R] Brand name — exact spelling and case. Any tagline? Is the
  tagline part of the mark or separate?
- [R] What does the company or project *do*, in one sentence?
- [R] Values and personality in exactly 3 words.
- [R] Where must the logo work — favicon at 16px, app icon, website,
  print, signage, embroidery, single colour, reversed on dark?
- [R] Colour constraints, and colours that must be avoided (competitor
  colours, cultural meanings, print limits)?
- Existing marks to keep or kill — what came before, and why it is
  being replaced?
- Competitors' marks to differ from — name them and say how.
- Preferred mark type, or none: wordmark, monogram, icon, combination
  mark, emblem? If "none", say so explicitly.
- Has any trademark or availability check been done? Never invent a
  check. If none was done, say so in the deliverable.
- What feeling should it give in 3 seconds — trustworthy, playful,
  premium, rebellious, calm, technical?
- Any words, letters, or symbols that must appear — or must never
  appear?

## How to ask

One message, numbered, plain language. Example opening: "Before I
design anything, I need these answers — a logo guessed from a vague
brief is how generic marks happen." If the user answers only some,
list the gaps back and ask whether to proceed on stated assumptions
or wait.
