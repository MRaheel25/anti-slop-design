# Intake — Landing Page / Website

Ask ALL questions in ONE message. Do not design with gaps. Record
unanswered items as explicit assumptions to confirm, never as silent
inventions. Required questions are marked [R].

## Universal (every type)

- [R] What is it for — what is the one action the viewer should take
  after seeing this?
- [R] Who is it for (audience, age, context, where they will meet it)?
- [R] Where will it be seen (sizes, surfaces, light and dark contexts)?
- [R] What exists already (brand, logo, colours, fonts, photos, copy)?
  Attach files or links.
- [R] What must it NOT look like, and who are the competitors to
  differ from?
- [R] What is the deadline and the deliverable format (file types,
  sizes, variants)?
- What is unknown — and who can answer it?

## Web-specific

- [R] The one job of the page, and the one action — sign up, buy,
  book, download, contact. Exactly one primary action.
- [R] Audience, and the objection to overcome — price, trust, time,
  complexity, "is this for me?"
- [R] Sections needed: hero, proof, how it works, pricing, FAQ,
  footer — and which of these are truly needed?
- [R] Real proof: numbers, testimonials with names, client logos
  with permission. Attach them. No invented proof, ever.
- Pricing and offer details — exact prices, plans, terms, guarantees.
- Existing site, brand, or DESIGN.md to keep — link it.
- Tech stack and hosting: static HTML, Next.js, Webflow, WordPress,
  other? Who will maintain it after delivery?
- SEO, meta, and Open Graph needs: title, description, social image?
- Analytics and form destination — real. Never a fake "thanks" form.
  Where does a submission actually go?
- Content: who writes the copy, and is it final? Long pages need
  real copy, not lorem.
- Accessibility bar: WCAG AA? Keyboard navigation? Reduced motion?

## How to ask

One message, numbered, plain language. A landing page without one
job, one action, one objection, and real proof is a brochure. Get
those four before researching.
