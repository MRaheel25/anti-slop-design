# Intake — Brand / Graphic

Ask ALL questions in ONE message. Do not design with gaps. Record
unanswered items as explicit assumptions to confirm, never as silent
inventions. Required questions are marked [R].

## Universal (every type)

- [R] What is it for — what is the one action the viewer should take
  after seeing this?
- [R] Who is it for (audience, age, context, where they will meet it)?
- [R] Where will it be seen (sizes, surfaces, light and dark contexts)?
- [R] What exists already (brand, logo, colours, fonts, photos, copy)?
  Attach files or links.
- [R] What must it NOT look like, and who are the competitors to
  differ from?
- [R] What is the deadline and the deliverable format (file types,
  sizes, variants)?
- What is unknown — and who can answer it?

## Brand / graphic-specific

- [R] Brand story in 2–3 sentences, and brand voice (formal,
  playful, luxury, technical, warm, rebellious — pick, do not hedge)?
- [R] Full deliverables list: stationery, slide deck, packaging,
  social templates, guidelines document, signage, merchandise?
- [R] Primary and secondary palette — existing hex values, or free
  choice? Any colour that is already owned by the brand?
- Type licences already owned — which fonts, and may they be used
  in this deliverable?
- Imagery style: photography, illustration, 3D, none — and whose
  photos or illustrations (supplied, stock, to be made)?
- Logo files available (vector, colour, mono, reversed)? Who owns
  the logo?
- Accessibility needs (contrast, legibility, language, reading level)?
- Print versus digital specs: bleed, CMYK versus RGB, paper, sizes,
  finishes?
- Where will the guidelines live, and who must be able to follow
  them without a designer present?
- Competitor brands to differ from — name them and say how.
- What does "done" look like — a PDF guideline, a template set, a
  single poster, a full identity?

## How to ask

One message, numbered, plain language. Brand work without a story,
a voice, and a deliverables list becomes decoration. Get those three
before researching.
