# Intake — Ad Creative

Ask ALL questions in ONE message. Do not design with gaps. Record
unanswered items as explicit assumptions to confirm, never as silent
inventions. Required questions are marked [R].

## Universal (every type)

- [R] What is it for — what is the one action the viewer should take
  after seeing this?
- [R] Who is it for (audience, age, context, where they will meet it)?
- [R] Where will it be seen (sizes, surfaces, light and dark contexts)?
- [R] What exists already (brand, logo, colours, fonts, photos, copy)?
  Attach files or links.
- [R] What must it NOT look like, and who are the competitors to
  differ from?
- [R] What is the deadline and the deliverable format (file types,
  sizes, variants)?
- What is unknown — and who can answer it?

## Ad-specific

- [R] Objective and KPI: click, install, purchase, awareness,
  sign-up — and how will success be measured?
- [R] Audience, targeting, and placement list: feed, story, display —
  with exact pixel sizes for every placement.
- [R] Headline, body copy, and CTA — exact approved wording, and who
  approves it? Attach it. Never rewrite approved legal copy.
- [R] Offer, price, and deadline — exact and verifiable. No invented
  discounts, no fake urgency.
- Brand and legal or compliance constraints: claims allowed, claims
  banned, disclaimers required, no before-and-after, no medical or
  financial promises?
- Competitors' ads to beat — link them and say what makes them work.
- Variants needed: how many sizes, how many hooks, how many
  concepts? One concept in many sizes is not the same as many hooks.
- Budget and run dates, if they change the design (for example a
  sale ending on a date that must be on the creative).
- What proof exists — reviews, ratings, numbers, awards — with
  sources and permission to use them?

## How to ask

One message, numbered, plain language. An ad without an objective,
an audience, approved copy, and a verifiable offer is a poster that
spends money. Get those four before researching. Never invent an
offer, a price, or a claim.
