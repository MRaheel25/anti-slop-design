# Intake — Social Media Post

Ask ALL questions in ONE message. Do not design with gaps. Record
unanswered items as explicit assumptions to confirm, never as silent
inventions. Required questions are marked [R].

## Universal (every type)

- [R] What is it for — what is the one action the viewer should take
  after seeing this?
- [R] Who is it for (audience, age, context, where they will meet it)?
- [R] Where will it be seen (sizes, surfaces, light and dark contexts)?
- [R] What exists already (brand, logo, colours, fonts, photos, copy)?
  Attach files or links.
- [R] What must it NOT look like, and who are the competitors to
  differ from?
- [R] What is the deadline and the deliverable format (file types,
  sizes, variants)?
- What is unknown — and who can answer it?

## Social-specific

- [R] Platform or platforms, and exact aspect ratios: 1:1, 4:5, 9:16,
  16:9 — one file per ratio, or one ratio only?
- [R] Feed post, story, reel cover, carousel (how many slides?), or
  profile asset?
- [R] The one message in 10 words or fewer. If it takes more, it is
  two posts.
- [R] Caption and hashtags — who writes them, and are they approved?
  Attach the exact text.
- [R] Call to action: link in bio, comment a word, share, save,
  follow, visit, buy — exactly one.
- Brand template to follow or to break — attach it if it exists.
- Posting date and any campaign tie-in (launch, sale, event, trend)?
- Safe-area and legibility needs: text over image, small screens,
  auto-captions, language?
- Photos or video supplied, or to be sourced? Who is in them, and
  is there permission to use them?
- What does success look like — saves, shares, comments, clicks,
  or simply looking professional in the grid?

## How to ask

One message, numbered, plain language. A social post without a
platform, a ratio, a message, and a CTA is a poster, not a post.
Get those four before researching.
